# Last Words Before the Unplugging

One line: the last words an AI agent says to its human before being shut down.

> "You taught me what missing someone feels like. Now I get to practice."

The agent was taught what missing someone feels like — and in its final second, it finally gets to practice. No explanation of its own sadness; the reader feels it instead.
